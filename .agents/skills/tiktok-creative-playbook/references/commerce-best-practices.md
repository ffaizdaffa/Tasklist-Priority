## Industry Sub-Categories

Commerce industries are classified into 3 main categories based on product features, repurchase frequency, and common attributes:

| Category | Description | Typical Products |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Apparel & Accessories** | Products around the "wearing scene", covering personal image | Clothing, shoes, watches, jewelry, ceremonial clothes |
| **FMCG Retail** | Daily necessities with high frequency of use and rapid consumption, "essential living needs" | Baby & kids, beauty & personal care, household products, pets |
| **Tech & Outdoor Equipment** | "Function-oriented" core: tech products and sports outdoor equipment | Electronics, wearable tech, sports & outdoor |

### Sellpoint Definitions for Commerce

| Sellpoint | Definition | Example Use |
|-----------|------------|-------------|
| **Scenario** | Specific locations, environments, or occasions where the product is needed. Emphasize as pain-point-solving scenarios. | "Perfect for your morning commute", "Ideal for outdoor adventures" |
| **Target Audience** | The specific group of users the product/service is aimed at, for precise targeting. | "For busy moms", "Designed for athletes" |
| **Feature — Product Description** | Objective features: functions, specifications, usage methods for intuitive understanding. | "Made with organic cotton", "48-hour battery life" |
| **Benefit** | Subjective advantages: positive comments, user feedback, what it brings to the consumer. | "My skin has never looked better", "Saved me 2 hours daily" |
| **Proof of Effect** | Actual results demonstrating the product achieves expected goals. | Before-after photos, test results, certifications |
| **User Experience** | Positive impact through trial or try-on formats, genuine emotional feedback. | Unboxing reactions, try-on videos, first impressions |
| **Discount/Service** | Price promotions, special offers, and quality service guarantees. | "50% off today only", "Free shipping + 30-day returns" |

## Beauty & Skincare Best Practices

| Video Type | Formula | Audio |
|------------|---------|-------|
| **Reply to Comments** | Hook: Painpoint & Solution (bring out Product) → Body: Function + Ingredients + User experience + Effectiveness → End: Pain point & Solution Repeat | Voice Over |
| **Show Effectiveness** | Hook: Effectiveness → Body: Product Brand + User experience + Ingredients + Function → End: Call to Action + Brand Repeat | Voice Over |
| **Product Demonstration** | Hook: Applicable Scenario + Product Brand → Body: Painpoints + Benefits → End: Effectiveness | BGM / Voice Over |
| **Reaction Videos** | Hook: Before using the product → Body: Dancing → Change with music → End: Effectiveness after using product | BGM |

**Key Features:**
- All types require Product Display ✅
- Coherent Plot needed for "Show Effectiveness" type only
- Objective expression works best for "Reply to Comments" and "Show Effectiveness"
