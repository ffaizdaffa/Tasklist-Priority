## Non-Commerce Sellpoint Framework

In Non-Commerce industries, most services are highly specialized. Use the following methods to help audiences quickly understand product advantages:

### User Experience

Successful customer cases enhance audience trust in the brand. Real feedback and user stories increase confidence in purchasing or accepting the service.

### Core Sellpoint Types

| Sellpoint Type | Description | Example Values |
|---------------|-------------|----------------|
| **User Benefit** (subjective) | Resolved distressing problems or achieved a better life | "Resolved unemployment crisis", "Improved quality of life", "Gained psychological satisfaction" |
| **Applicable Scenario / Target Audience** | Social identity, age, preferences, situational needs | "If you are also in the __ situation", "When you have the trouble and need of __" |
| **Benefits** | Tangible advantages of the service | "Affordable price", "High-paying jobs after graduation", "Personalized 1-on-1 support", "Official certifications" |
| **Allay Concerns** | Remove barriers to action | "Simple registration", "No qualification verification needed", "Zero handling fees", "Certified by authoritative institutions" |

### Expression

Voice-over is the most common approach. For UGC-style videos without voice-over, add captions or text stickers to display key selling points.

## Education

### Key Selling Points
- **High Cost-Effectiveness**: Easy entry (no prior qualifications needed), quick and affordable process, high returns ($XXX/week jobs after graduation)
- **Product Advantages**: Well-designed courses and rich teaching resources
- **Additional Services**: Internship opportunities, complimentary services, brand partnerships
- **Authoritative Endorsement**: Certified by professional/national institutions
- **User Feedback**: "Completed degree in XX days." Supervisor: "XX transformed my career."
- **Target Audience**: Career-confused individuals, students, self-improvement seekers

## Healthcare & Pharmaceutical

In professional, price-sensitive industries, use consumer stories and expert images to boost credibility.
- **Hook**: Focus on price and quality of life
- **Selling Points**: Affordable costs, expert advice, cutting-edge equipment. Positive feedback is common.
- **CTA**: Use quizzes or exclusive consultations to get quotes and guidance.

### Healthcare Video Formulas

#### Pharmaceutical

| Type | Idea | Formula |
|------|------|---------|
| **Narrative** (third-party perspective) | Marketing videos | Hook Painpoint + Effect + Sellpoints + User experience + Low-cost CTA |
| **Storytelling** (consumer perspective) | Real user experience / Tip sharing | Debunking rumors Hook / Sharing tips + Low cost & benefit CTA + User experience / POV (eliciting anxiety) + Solution + CTA repeat |
| **Storytelling** (professional perspective) | FAQ Reply | Hot topics + Professional advice + Selling points + CTA with solution plan |

#### Hospitals & Healthcare Service Providers

| Type | Idea | Formula |
|------|------|---------|
| **Narrative** (professional perspective) | Peer price comparison, cost performance | [Hook: Anxiety/Pain Point] + Solution/Product + Selling Point + [Urgent Call to Action] |
| **Narrative** (consumer perspective) | Personal experience | Effect + User experience + Promotion price |
