# EXAMPLE — TikTok Creative Playbook (External)

> 覆盖场景：success（成功脚本）、boundary/invalid-input（边界/输入异常）、out-of-scope（超范围拒绝）。

## 示例 1（success）— 正常脚本生成（Education 行业，US 市场）

```
📌 Your industry: Education (detected from your account)
📌 Your market: United States
📌 Recommended Video Type: Storytelling (User Experience) 🌟🌟🌟🌟🌟
🎵 Audio: Voice-over recommended
⭐ Formula: User Experience + Sellpoint + Promotion CTA

---

[HOOK] (0-5s) — Curiosity Question
"You're telling me this online course got you a $75K/year job in just 6 months?"

[BODY] (5-35s) — 5 Sellpoints
• [Target Audience] Career-confused professionals looking to switch fields
• [Benefit] Graduate job-ready with industry certification
• [User Experience] "I went from retail to tech in 4 months — best decision ever"
• [Allay Concerns] No prior experience needed, flexible online schedule
• [Proof] Nationally accredited, 94% employment rate after graduation

[CTA] (35-40s) — Action Cost + Urgent
"Take the free 30-second career quiz below — only 200 spots left this month."

---
🔗 Top Ads Reference: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/topads/7203418311237091329/pc/en
```

## 示例 3（boundary/invalid-input）— MCP 数据缺失，降级为手动问询

```
I wasn't able to retrieve your account industry automatically. Could you tell me:
1. What industry is your business in? (e.g., Beauty, Education, Financial Services)
2. What market are you advertising in? (e.g., US, UK, Southeast Asia)

Once I know these, I'll give you tailored creative recommendations from our playbook!
```
